Scholarly core: math with KaTeX or MathJax (shared macros, `\label` numbering, MathML), numbered figures and tables with the `Figure` component, cross-references (`@fig:`, `@tbl:`, `@eq:`, `@sec:`), citations from a BibTeX file with a Distill-style bibliography, footnotes as sidenotes, hover cards for citations and footnotes, a generated appendix with a citation box and BibTeX, the `Acknowledgments`, `Contributions` and `AppendixSection` components, and Highwire and Open Graph meta tags.
